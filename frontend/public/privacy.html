Privacy Policy

Collab-docs · Last updated 1 September 2026

Collab-docs is a personal, non-commercial project demonstrating collaborative text editing. This policy describes exactly what the application stores, what it does not, and how Google account data is handled. It is written to match what the code actually does.

What is stored when you sign in

You can sign in with an email and password, or with Google. In either case the application stores:

  • Your email address and display name.
  • A randomly assigned presence colour, used to identify you to collaborators in a shared document.
  • A session token, stored only as a SHA-256 hash, which expires 30 days after it is issued.
  • If you signed in with Google, the stable subject identifier Google issues for your account. This is what links your sign-in to your account. The email address is not used as the key, because addresses can change.

Your Google profile picture is not stored. If you signed in with Google, no password is stored for your account at all.

What is stored when you use the editor

  • The content of documents you create, as an append-only log of edits plus periodic snapshots.
  • Comments, tasks and tab names, together with the display name of whoever wrote them.
  • Sharing permissions — which accounts may read or edit a document.

Because edits are stored as an append-only log, earlier versions of a document remain recoverable through version history until the document is deleted.

Google Drive

The optional Import from Drive feature uses the drive.file scope. This is the narrowest Drive permission Google offers, and it was chosen deliberately:

  • It grants access only to the individual files you personally select through Google's own file picker. The application cannot list, browse, search or read anything else in your Drive.
  • The access token is obtained in your browser and stays there. It is never transmitted to, or stored on, this application's servers. This application's servers never contact Google Drive.
  • No refresh token is requested. The access token expires on its own, typically within an hour, and cannot be renewed without you granting permission again. The application has no ability to read your Drive when you are not present.
  • An import is a one-time copy. The selected document is exported once, converted, and becomes an ordinary document here. Your original file in Drive is never modified, never written to, and is not linked to the copy.

Collab-docs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used solely to provide the sign-in and document import features described above.

What is not done

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party trackers.
  • Your data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No data is used to train machine learning models.

Where data is held

The application runs on Render and stores data in a Neon PostgreSQL database. These providers process data solely to host the service. Traffic is served over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual documents from within the application. To delete your account and everything associated with it, email the address below and it will be removed from the database.

You can revoke this application's access to your Google account at any time, and independently of anything above, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

This project is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
