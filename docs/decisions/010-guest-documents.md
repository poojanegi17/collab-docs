# ADR-010: Anyone may write, only an account may share

**Status:** accepted

**Consequence:** A document made without an account is open to anyone holding the link.

Giving documents owners implied a rule nobody actually decided: sign up before
you can write anything. Meanwhile the thing that genuinely needs an account —
sharing — could only say "the owner may share", which on a document with no
owner is true and useless.

Creating and editing need no account. The document is simply unowned, which was
already a state the system understood, so nothing new had to be invented beside
it.

Sharing needs an account, because of what sharing is: granting access writes
down a person, so there has to be a person at both ends. That is the one place
in the product where the sign-in wall stands.

Claiming is the bridge, walked in place — sign in, claim, share — and it costs
something that is said out loud. Anyone else editing anonymously is
disconnected when a document gains an owner, and the message names the reason.
Documents made without an account are remembered on the device, as a
convenience list rather than a record of who may open them.
